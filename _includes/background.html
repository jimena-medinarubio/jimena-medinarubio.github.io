About me

I'm currently a PhD Candidate of Physical Oceanography @ the Institute of Marine and Atmospheric Research (Utrecht University). I graduated in Physics from Imperial College London with a *First Class Honours*. I got interested in the field of Geophysical Fluid Dynamics after spending a year abroad at UU in 2023, I had the opportunity to take courses from the Climate Physics master's program like Dynamical Meteorology, Dynamical Oceanography, Atmospheric Composition & Chemical Processes & GFD. Back at Imperial in 2024, I dived deeper into the scope of the field by taking additional courses in Hydrodynamics, Space & Plasma Physics, Atmospheric Physics, and Vortex Dynamics.

My research explores the transport and dispersal of macroplastics in the North Sea. I am interested in using Lagrangian modelling tools, such as Parcels, and machine learning techniques to trace the trajectories of buoyant plastics at the ocean surface and analyse the impact of different forcing mechanisms on their motion and accumulation patterns.
